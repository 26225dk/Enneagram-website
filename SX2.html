"Seduction"

Sexual 2

Don't let the title fool you! The "sexual" instinct has nothing to do with literal sex. Instead, it is another term for "chemistry energy" and it is used to describe the intensity of one-on-one connections with people you love. Think of it like a magnetic pull that draws people in.

An example of how sx2 could act:

They are the student who becomes very close to one person fast and wants to know everything about them, from their favorite music to their biggest fears. They make people feel special, needed, and emotionally seen, often becoming very attached to the people they care about. They are warm and intense, but they may become needy or possessive when they feel ignored or unimportant.
