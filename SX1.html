"Zeal"

Sexual 1

Don't let the title fool you! The "sexual" instinct has nothing to do with literal sex. Rather, it's another term for "chemistry energy" and it's used to describe the intensity of one-on-one connections with the people you love, like a magnetic pull that draws people in.

An example of how sx1 could act:

They are the student who holds themselves to impossible standards and feels deeply uncomfortable when they think they have been lazy or morally wrong. In close friendships or relationships, they can be intense, principled, and very serious about doing what is right. They care deeply about integrity, but they can also become rigid or overly self-critical when they feel they have fallen short.
