Heart Triad
These types are mainly focused on their self-image and desire for validation, either externalized (2), denied (3), or internalized (4).

Head Triad
These types are mainly focused on fear, thinking, and their desire for security, either internalized (5), externalized (6), or denied (7).

Gut Triad
These types are mainly focused on their anger, either externalized (8), denied (9), or internalized (1).
