"Fascination"

Sexual 7

Don't let the title fool you! The "sexual" instinct has nothing to do with literal sex. Instead, it is another term for "chemistry energy" and it is used to describe the intensity of one-on-one connections with people you love. Think of it like a magnetic pull that draws people in.

An example of how sx7 could act:

They are the student who makes everything more exciting, whether it is a school trip, a group project, or a boring day in class. They are full of ideas, energy, and spontaneity, and they often bring people together by making life feel more fun and less limited. They love freedom and stimulation, but they may avoid serious emotional conversations if they feel they will slow things down.
