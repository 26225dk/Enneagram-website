Social 1

An example of how so1 could act:

They are the captain of the debate team or the lead in a school play. They have high energy and keep track of everyone's names, also keeping track of who has the highest grades and who is the most popular in class. During a group assignment they immediately take charge, assign the best roles to the top students, and stay up all night editing the final slieds just to guarantee their group gets the highest grade in the year level. Of all the enneagram three subtypes, they are the most likely to brag about their achievements and successes.
