TYPE 2:

THE HELPER

Enneagram twos have a need to be liked and appreciated by others. They value relationships which makes kindness, generosity, and self-sacrifice important to them. They strive to make the world a more loving place so they offer support and attention to those they care about. At their best, they are unconditionally supportive, practice self care, and offer the gift of humility to themselves and others. At their worst, they may seem flattering or manipulative as they "give to get" which is motivated by belief that they don't deserve to be loved for who they are.
