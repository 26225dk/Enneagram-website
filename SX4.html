"Hate"

Sexual 4

Don't let the title fool you! The "sexual" instinct has nothing to do with literal sex. Instead, it is another term for "chemistry energy" and it is used to describe the intensity of one-on-one connections with people you love. Think of it like a magnetic pull that draws people in.

An example of how sx4 could act:

They are the student who writes the most personal essay, picks the most emotional topic, or makes a project that feels like a piece of themselves. They want to be truly understood and often feel things with a lot of intensity, especially in close relationships. They are expressive and deep, but they can get overwhelmed by emotion or become dramatic when they feel unseen.
