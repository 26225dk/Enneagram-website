"Totem"

Social 5

Social fives search for the meaning in situations and place huge focus on complex questions to pursue wisdom and knowledge. They connect with groups or experts who share their brilliant ideas while staying disconnected from their everyday problems or emotions. Even though they share their ideas with energy and enthusiasm, they can resist sharing their space, time, or resources as they disconnect from those around them.

An example of how so5 could act:

They are the friend who becomes an expert on a specific topic like coding, history, or game lore. They have a quiet and observant energy but light up when talking with the school robotics club or chess team (or anything that highly interests them). During a group project, they prefer to be the research engine who researches the facts while the group relies on them as the smartest person in the room for that topic.
