Don't let the title fool you! The "social" instinct has nothing to do with literal socializing. Instead, it is another term for "chemistry energy" and it is used to describe the intensity of one-on-one connections with people you love. Think of it like a magnetic pull that draws people in.

An example of how sx5 could act:
