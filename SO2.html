"Ambition"

Social 2

Social twos use their charm on a smart level to attract attention from communities or social gatherings. They love being the centre of attention and easily step into leadership roles because of this. Sometimes they tend to give more than they receive which is a way to distract themselves from their own difficult feelings. Since they can seem less childlike than the other twos, they can be mistyped (mistaken themselves) as enneagram 3 or 8.

An example of how so2 could act:

They are the friend who is a student council president or a club leader. They have high energy and knows everyone's name while trying to take care of everyone in the classroom by organizing group study sessions.
